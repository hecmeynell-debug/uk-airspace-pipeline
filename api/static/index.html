UK Airspace Pipeline

Observed traffic density over the UK FIRs and eastern North Atlantic, from public ADS-B data.

Aggregate and descriptive only. No individual aircraft tracking. No intent prediction or threat scoring. All data is public.

Traffic density

Observations per 1° grid cell, last 24h. Cells, not positions — deliberately too coarse to locate anything.

Altitude bands

Share of observations by band, last 24h.

Observations over time

Hourly totals. Gaps are gaps — a quiet hour and an outage look the same here, which is why pipeline health is shown separately.

Pipeline health

The pipeline, not the airspace. Runs, error rates, latency and API credit spend.

Limitations

  • Platform engineering demonstration only. Not an operational system.
  • Data from the OpenSky Network, used under its research and non-commercial terms.
